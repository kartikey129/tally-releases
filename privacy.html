Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What Tally stores

Tally keeps the shop's bills, stock, customers and settings on the shop's own computer. Nothing is sent to the makers of Tally, and Tally has no server of its own.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When the shop connects it, Tally asks Google only for the drive.file permission. With it, Tally can see and change only the files it creates itself, and none of the shop's other Drive files.

  • Each bill the shop shares on WhatsApp is saved as a PDF in a "Tally Bills" folder in the shop's own Google Drive.
  • The file is shared as view-only for anyone with its link, so the customer can open the bill. Nobody else can change or delete it.
  • Estimate PDFs are deleted from the Drive after 90 days. Tax invoices stay until the shop deletes them.
  • The Google sign-in is stored encrypted on the shop's computer. The shop can disconnect at any time in Tally's Settings, or remove Tally from its Google account's third-party access.

Tally's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to save and share the shop's own bills. It is not sold, used for advertising, or given to anyone else.

Updates

To check for new versions, Tally reads the public list of releases on GitHub. This sends no shop data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the shop that uses Tally, or open an issue on github.com/kartikey129/tally-releases.
